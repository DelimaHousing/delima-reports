The story this month

August generated Rp 79.99M in total revenue — the best month of the year so far, up 8% MoM vs July.

Nine paid OTA nights contributed Rp 26.75M, while 16 direct-booked nights (Jana Lucas 15n + Jana Direct 1n) added Rp 47.45M. Two cancelled OTA nights (dates blocked, fees retained) added Rp 5.80M. Property-wide ADR landed at Rp 2,963k across 27 occupied nights.

Net profit landed at ~Rp 50.0M with a 62.5% margin after the yearly Internet prepay (Rp 2.93M, one-time) is booked in Aug — rate discipline held and the tax base excludes direct bookings.

Net profit landed at Rp 50.0M vs Rp 39.7M in July — a Rp 10.3M step up driven by direct-booking revenue (no OTA commission and excluded from the 10% government tax base), net of the Rp 2.93M yearly Internet prepay booked in Aug.

Margin lifted from 54%→65.8% because direct bookings (Rp 47.45M) bypassed the ~17% Airbnb commission and are also excluded from the 10% government tax base. Operational expenses came in at ~Rp 7.34M once Aug laundry was booked.
