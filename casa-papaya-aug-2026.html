The story this month

August delivered Rp 53.30M in total revenue — essentially flat vs July's Rp 53.28M. Ten paying stays across the month kept the villa near-full at 84% occupancy.

OTA carried the entire month: Rp 49.9M from 9 paid Airbnb stays (25 nights), plus Rp 1.9M retained cancellation fee. Direct contribution stayed light — the Sheena Aug 2–3 direct is currently held at Rp 1.5M pending final confirmation.

Net profit landed at Rp 28.34M (53.2% margin). Op-ex came in at Rp 5.54M, in line with recent monthly rhythm; Guest Supplies stepped up to Rp 1.11M (vs Rp 0.37M in Jul) driven by turnover volume.
